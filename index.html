42+ Years of Excellence

Our Rich Scholastic Legacy

MAEER's MIT Group Pune has built a distinguished education legacy of more than 42 years. Our institutional network includes 4 universities and over 70 institutions, educating a combined cohort of 70,000+ active students, guided by expert faculty, and supported by a global community of over 200,000+ alumni.

At MIT Junior Colleges, Alandi, we extend this powerful legacy to junior college education (Class 11th and 12th). Located Opp. Gajanan Maharaj Temple, Alandi (D), our campus offers students a robust value-based academic environment designed to convert potential into high performance.

We blend the official HSC board curriculum with integrated coaching for entrance exams (JEE, NEET, MHT-CET, CA, and CS Foundation), preparing students not just for board exams, but for competitive success and rewarding careers.

Step-by-Step Guide

Application Timeline

Follow our four-step process to enroll in your desired academic stream.

Step 01

Admission Enquiry

Fill out the online callback form on our website or visit the Alandi campus. Select your stream interest (Science or Commerce) and opt-in for competitive coaching.

Step 02

Document Submission

Submit your Class 10th board marksheet, school leaving certificate, Aadhaar card copy, and passport size photographs for administrative verification.

Step 03

Stream & Coaching Selection

Meet with our expert academic counsellors to evaluate your career objectives and choose the appropriate integrated program.

Step 04

Seat Confirmation

Receive your admission confirmation card. Secure your seat by completing the fee payment and submitting physical documents.
